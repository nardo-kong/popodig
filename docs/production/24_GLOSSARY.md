# 24 — Project Glossary

## Dig
The dominant player verb: designate solid legal terrain for mouse excavation.

## Decision Frontier
The boundary between excavated space and unknown solid terrain where the player decides how to expand.

## Spatial Gradient
A nonuniform property that makes possible directions unequal: Information, Value, Risk, Cost, or Topology.

## Decision Knot
A locally generated arrangement where multiple directions present different combinations of spatial gradients. It creates thought without forcing route choices.

## Discovery Feature / Discovery Object
A major underground content object following Signal → Reveal → Reaction → Adaptation → Stable State.

## Signal Field
Hidden numeric/logical field that produces qualitative clues around an unknown feature, such as moisture or root density.

## Reveal
The moment excavation/exposure makes a hidden feature known.

## Reaction
The immediate systemic consequence following reveal.

## Adaptation
Player response using topology, zones, seal/support, or further digging.

## Stable State
The persistent resolved form of a discovery after immediate disruption ends.

## Stratum
A broad depth/environment band with overlapping materials/features and a distinct rules emphasis; not a separate level.

## Landmark
A guaranteed macro world structure used for progression, story, or spatial orientation.

## Feature
A structured world element such as Root Cluster, Water Reservoir, Natural Cavity, or Old Burrow.

## World Skeleton
The macro generation stage defining dimensions, start, depth bands, landmark regions, and chapter framework.

## Structured PCG
Semi-procedural generation where authored rules/templates establish guarantees and randomization varies local shape/placement.

## Validate → Repair
Post-generation process that checks playability/design invariants and minimally modifies/retries invalid worlds.

## Tile-level simulation
Discrete cell-based approximation used for water, stability, environment, and terrain rather than pixel/continuous physics.

## Zone
Player-assigned purpose applied to excavated cells, e.g. Sleeping or Storage.

## Autonomous Colony
Mice choose and execute available jobs according to needs, urgency, distance, and priorities without routine direct player control.

## Knowledge Progression
Growth in the player's and colony UI's ability to interpret underground signals, rather than solely numerical upgrades.

## Spatial Consequence Sandbox
Internal descriptor for the design: player freedom changes persistent geometry, and changed geometry affects future life/decisions.
