# 04 — World Structure & Chapters

**Status: LOCKED macro structure / PROVISIONAL dimensions**

## 1. World premise
The complete V1 world exists beneath one old house and adjoining garden. It is a continuous side-view vertical cross-section.

The player begins near the surface and gradually establishes a deeper, safer home.

## 2. Finite-world principle
The world is finite for four reasons:
1. quality validation is possible;
2. key feature relationships can be guaranteed;
3. performance/save boundaries are clear;
4. the campaign can have a designed conclusion.

Finite does not mean linear. The player has broad horizontal/vertical freedom inside the world.

## 3. Recommended logical dimensions
**PROVISIONAL starting target:**
- Cell size: 16 logical pixels for simulation/art prototype; final rendering scale may use higher-resolution sprites.
- Vertical Slice: about 96 × 72 cells.
- Full V1 campaign: about 160–240 cells wide × 180–260 cells deep.

Do not lock final dimensions until traversal and zoom tests validate readability.

## 4. World layers
### Surface band
Visual context only plus limited entry interactions. Garden/house hints establish location.

### Stratum A — Topsoil / Settle
- soft soil;
- small stones;
- roots;
- simple natural cavities;
- first food opportunities.

### Stratum B — Root & Moisture Transition
- denser roots;
- damp gradients;
- clay bands;
- first meaningful route tradeoffs.

### Stratum C — Wet/Foundation / Adapt
- reservoirs;
- unstable pockets;
- stone/brick foundation;
- pipes/heat/sound signals where used.

### Stratum D — Old Burrow / Endgame
- pre-existing cavities;
- collapsed legacy passages;
- old nesting traces;
- stable warm region and final objective.

## 5. Strata are gradients, not hard level boundaries
Boundaries should be irregular and overlap. The player may see features from the next stratum before formally reaching its chapter phase.

Avoid visual “biome bands” that feel like unrelated levels stacked vertically.

## 6. Chapter gating
Chapter transitions occur through milestone knowledge/objectives rather than invisible walls.

Example:
- Chapter 2 begins when the colony is stable enough and reaches the moisture/foundation depth range.
- Chapter 3 begins when traces of the old burrow are discovered.

Players may still excavate sideways/backward.

## 7. Landmark system
Landmarks are guaranteed macro structures used to anchor story and world legibility.

V1 candidates:
- Start Burrow
- Major Root Anchor
- First Reservoir Region
- House Foundation Segment
- Old Burrow Entrance
- Final Warm Chamber

Landmark positions vary within authored placement constraints.

## 8. Spatial storytelling
Narrative is delivered through world evidence rather than frequent dialogue:
- old chew marks;
- bundled grass remnants;
- sealed/failed tunnels;
- foundation damage;
- abandoned storage traces;
- safe chamber shapes.

Do not require elaborate lore exposition to understand objectives.

## 9. Surface relationship
The surface should remain visually meaningful as a reference point. Depth communicates progress.

Potential surface changes are cosmetic/seasonal in V1 and should not become a separate farming game.

## 10. World readability
At zoomed-out view, the player should distinguish:
- excavated vs solid terrain;
- wet/water regions;
- major root masses;
- unstable warning areas;
- active zones;
- major landmarks.

At close view, signals and mouse life provide detail.

## 11. Story outline
### Act I — A place to sleep
A tiny colony seeks shelter and food beneath the garden.

### Act II — The ground has its own shape
Deeper excavation reveals that water, weak soil, roots, and human structures determine where a home can safely grow.

### Act III — Someone lived here before
The colony discovers an old burrow network and traces showing prior mice faced the same underground constraints.

### Resolution — Make it home
The player rehabilitates/connects a stable warm chamber before seasonal conditions fully worsen.

## 12. Post-completion sandbox
After completion:
- objectives stop applying pressure;
- player may keep excavating and reorganizing;
- no infinite terrain extension is required.
